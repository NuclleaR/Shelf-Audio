# Privacy Policy

Shelf Audio is an audiobook player. It has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no account. Your audiobooks,
your progress, your bookmarks and your listening history stay on your device and in your own folders. Shelf Audio
collects no personal data from anyone, children included, and the developer receives none of it. This page lists every
case where Shelf Audio sends something off your device, who gets it and why.

## Cover search

When you search for a cover online, Shelf Audio sends your search, which starts as the book's name, to DuckDuckGo. The
previews come from DuckDuckGo, and the cover you pick is downloaded from the website it's on.

## Audiobookshelf

You can connect Shelf Audio to an Audiobookshelf server that you or someone you trust runs. Shelf Audio then sends your
username and password to that server to sign in, and exchanges your listening progress, bookmarks and listening
sessions, including your device model and the app version, with it. It also streams and downloads audiobooks from it.
This data goes only to the server you entered. Signing out removes the books of the server and their downloads from
your device.

## Backups

If backups are on on your device, Android backs up Shelf Audio's data, like your library, progress and bookmarks, to
your backup account, on most devices your Google account. The login to an Audiobookshelf server isn't backed up.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: [open an issue](https://github.com/NuclleaR/Shelf-Audio/issues).

When Shelf Audio starts sending something new, this page changes with that release.
